### Project Idea

Espresso XR is an interactive virtual reality experience developed using the Godot Engine. The project aims to teach users the fundamental steps involved in preparing espresso using a traditional espresso machine.

The learner will be placed in a virtual coffee-making environment containing an espresso machine and the necessary equipment. Through hands-on interactions, they will learn how to prepare an espresso, from handling the portafilter and tamping the coffee grounds to operating the machine and extracting the espresso.

The project will focus on simulating the espresso-making process through simplified interactions rather than attempting to recreate the complex mechanical and physical behaviour of a real espresso machine.

### What Will the Learner Learn?

The experience aims to introduce learners to the basic techniques and equipment involved in espresso preparation.

By completing the experience, the learner should be able to:

- Identify essential espresso-making equipment and understand its purpose.
- Understand the importance of correctly dosing and tamping coffee grounds.
- Learn how to prepare and attach a portafilter to an espresso machine.
- Understand the basic extraction process and the importance of extraction time.
- Follow the correct sequence of steps required to prepare an espresso.

### How Will the Learner Learn?

The experience will use interactive, step-by-step instructions to guide the learner through the espresso-making process.

The learner will interact with virtual equipment using XR controllers, allowing them to pick up, position and use objects such as the portafilter, tamper and espresso cup.

The experience will initially guide learners through the preparation process, displaying instructions and visual feedback as they complete each step. Once familiar with the procedure, learners may attempt to prepare an espresso independently, with feedback provided based on their performance.

For example, attempting to extract espresso before the portafilter has been correctly prepared and attached could trigger a warning explaining the required steps.

### Planned Features

- A simplified interactive 3D espresso machine.
- Grabbable equipment, including a portafilter, tamper and espresso cup.
- Interactive coffee preparation steps, including dosing and tamping.
- A simulated espresso extraction process.
- Visual instructions and feedback throughout the experience.
- Sound effects and animations to make interactions more engaging.
- A potential independent practice mode.

### Technologies and Interaction Libraries

The project will be developed using the following technologies:

- **Godot Engine:** The primary game engine used to create the virtual environment, objects and interactions.
- **GDScript:** Used to implement the espresso preparation workflow, object interactions and learning feedback.
- **OpenXR:** Used to support the virtual reality experience on the Meta Quest.
- **Godot XR Tools:** Used to implement interactions such as grabbing, holding and positioning virtual objects.
- **Godot physics and collision detection:** Used to detect interactions between equipment and predefined areas.
